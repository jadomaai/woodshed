Play along just keeps time with a click while you read — nothing is graded, no mic needed.

Record & grade counts you in, clicks through the piece while it records you, then checks each note afterwards: it looks for a pluck near where that note should fall and checks the pitch. It only judges pitch and rough timing, not tone or which string you used.

This is a first cut at correctness-checking — live, note-by-note feedback while you play (rather than a report afterwards) is a possible future addition.
All generation, notation and pitch detection run locally in your browser. A take is buffered in memory only long enough to grade it, then discarded — nothing is saved or uploaded.
